## The product platform behind the studio

Doscientos is not just a marketing site. I built its digital product layer end to end, so the way we present the studio, run operations and reuse engineering work is one connected system.

### Public site
The public site presents the studio's work and gives its digital products a consistent home, with a clear content structure and reusable frontend patterns.

### Operations backoffice
The in-house backoffice centralises the daily workflow across companies, leads, clients, project tasks and invoicing. It gives the team a shared source of truth instead of a collection of disconnected tools and spreadsheets.

### Shared package ecosystem
Five reusable packages turn cross-product patterns into maintained building blocks. That reduces duplicated work, lets improvements reach every product centrally and keeps delivery consistent as the studio grows.

### MCP and specialised skills
I extended the operating stack with an MCP layer and specialised skills, so AI assistants can retrieve approved internal context and help with operational work where it happens. Access is scoped to the task rather than exposing raw business data.
