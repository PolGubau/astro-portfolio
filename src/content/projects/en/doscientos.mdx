## The product platform behind the studio

Doscientos is not just a marketing site. I built its digital product layer end to end, so the way we present the studio, run operations and reuse engineering work is one connected system.

### Marketing landing
The public site turns our positioning into a clear acquisition experience: what we build, how we work and why senior engineers stay involved from discovery to launch.

### Operations backoffice
The in-house backoffice centralises the daily workflow across companies, leads, clients, project tasks and invoicing. It gives the team a shared source of truth instead of a collection of disconnected tools and spreadsheets.

### Shared package ecosystem
Five reusable packages turn cross-product patterns into maintained building blocks. That reduces duplicated work, lets improvements reach every product centrally and keeps delivery consistent as the studio grows.

### MCP and specialised skills
I extended the operating stack with an MCP layer and specialised skills, so AI assistants can retrieve approved internal context and help with operational work where it happens. Access is scoped to the task rather than exposing raw business data.

## What we build

### Custom internal systems (CRMs, ERPs, portals)
For companies managing clients, orders, or teams in Excel or a generic CRM that doesn't match their process. The exact tool they need - no monthly licences, no adapting the business to a template.

### MVPs in 6 weeks
Booking platforms, marketplaces, B2B tools, internal management apps. We ship just enough to validate with real users - first customers are testing the product while competitors are still in Figma.

### Process automation
We connect stores, ERPs, CRMs, and accounting systems. Orders flow into the ERP automatically, invoices generate themselves, reports run without human intervention. Measurable ROI in 2-3 months.

### Legacy system modernisation
We migrate from Access, monolithic PHP, or Excel to modern architectures. While we build the new system, the old one keeps running. End users notice nothing - the business notices everything.
