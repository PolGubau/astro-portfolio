For **Golf Natural Guidance**, I built a working golf-academy management demo through [Doscientos](https://doscientos.es). Its goal was to coordinate instructors, schedules, students, activities, payments and invoices through one connected experience.

The demo brings together two surfaces: a public booking experience and a backoffice for running the business. It was not deployed to production, but it validated the full journey and left a navigable foundation for product decisions.

## The challenge

When booking, scheduling, payments and invoicing live in separate tools, the team becomes the bridge between systems. The student sees only part of the process, while the academy has to hold the entire operation together.

The challenge was to design one experience that simplified the operation without hiding the complexity behind it.

---

## The solution

The public experience guides students through choosing a service, instructor, date and time, entering their details and completing a simulated payment. It is mobile-first and keeps the next decision in focus.

The backoffice brings together the operational overview, schedule, clients, instructors, acquisition, automations and invoicing. Information is organised around the team's real tasks rather than the database's internal structure.

The architecture leaves room for real authentication, payments, notifications and tax integrations without making those future pieces a dependency for the initial product validation.

## My contribution
