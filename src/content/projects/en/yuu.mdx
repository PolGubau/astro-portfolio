## A custom portfolio for Yunaplena

Yu Vilaseca needed to move her portfolio from Cargo to an independent website while keeping the visual direction of **Yunaplena** and bringing together her work as an art director and DJ.

The rebuild shapes the site into an editorial experience that is easy to navigate: a photographic home page, a selection of projects, and dedicated pages for her profile and music activity.

<Image src={portrait} alt="Black-and-white portrait of Yu Vilaseca used on the Yunaplena home page" class="show-animation rounded-2xl my-8" />

### What it includes

- Full-screen home page with portrait, wordmark and links to the main sections.
- Portfolio with individual project pages and galleries for each project's images and videos.
- About and DJ & Music sections, with biography, event history and links to her channels.
- Fixed navigation that marks the current section, with responsive layouts and image zoom.

### From a visual editor to a site of her own

The site was rebuilt with **Astro**, with copy, projects and local media organised in the codebase instead of depending on Cargo's editor. The design retains the serif headlines, monochrome home-page composition and restrained interface, with subtle transitions.

The result is a custom, responsive static portfolio for presenting Yu Vilaseca's creative work.
